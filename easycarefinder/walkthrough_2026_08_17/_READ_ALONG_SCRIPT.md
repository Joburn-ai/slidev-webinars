## How to record this

**Open the deck on one screen and this document on your phone.** Advance the slide, read the line,
advance again. The words on the slides are deliberately few, so there is nothing to read off the
screen itself. **This is the script.**

Two other ways to get the same thing, whichever suits you:
- **The live deck**, press `p` for presenter mode. It shows the current slide, the next one, and
  these exact notes.
- **Google Slides**, View then Presenter view. The same notes are in there.

## Three rules while recording

1. **Read it as written.** The wording has been through a compliance pass. Where it says "a large
   part" rather than a number, that is deliberate and the number is not cleared yet.
2. **Never say a figure that is not on this page.** No count of homes, no staffing ratio, no price,
   no date, no day of the week. Nothing that would need re-recording later.
3. **Stop at any slide marked 🔴.** Those two are not cleared to record yet.
