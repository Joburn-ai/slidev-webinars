## T2: What a program actually is

**On-slide headline:** `WHAT A PROGRAM ACTUALLY IS`

**SAY:**
Alright, quick bit of education, because you gotta understand a couple of concepts before we get into the actual programs. So what in the world is a first-time buyer program? Well, the honest answer is, it depends, because they come in a few flavors.

Some are tied to the location of the home. Literally, if the house is on this side of the street I qualify, and on the other side of the street I don't, because of some arbitrary government map. Some depend on your income, and a lot of them have income caps, like sixty or seventy grand for a single person, which knocks you out if you're one of my higher earners. So the ones we care about here are the ones with really high income caps, or no income cap at all. Some ask you to hang onto the home for a couple of years. Some come with interest rates that are lower than market, which drops your monthly payment. And some, write this one down, are a hundred percent financing, which means no down payment at all, because the loan covers the whole thing.

Don't try to memorize all that. Just know the pieces move around. That's actually the good news, and you'll see why in a second.
