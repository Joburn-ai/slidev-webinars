## 2. Type

The deck runs on **three registers** (a signal of two build eras + pasted-in social cards — see §5). For the rebuild, standardize on Register A.

- **Display / headlines — heavy condensed grotesque, ALL CAPS.** Tall, tight, geometric caps ("PROGRAMS FOR HIGH-EARNING FIRST-TIME BUYERS", "MISTAKE #1:", "VETERANS AFFAIRS LOAN (VA)"). Closest web/Google equivalents: **Anton**, **Oswald (Bold/Heavy)**, or **Bebas Neue / Archivo Narrow Black**. This is the brand's shout.
- **Sub-head / name / wordmark — geometric rounded sans.** The "Bradley Pounds" name and the "Homebuyer" logo wordmark use rounded terminals → **Poppins (SemiBold/Bold)** family (or Quicksand). Warmer, friendlier.
- **Body — clean geometric-humanist sans.** Bullets, "Discover down payment assistance…", "Hello, I'm" → **Poppins (Regular/Medium)**-family. Legible, modern.

**Recommendation:** Anton/Oswald-Heavy for display + Poppins for everything else = a faithful, license-clean recreation of the house look.
