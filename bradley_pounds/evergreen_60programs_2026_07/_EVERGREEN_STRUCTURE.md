# EVERGREEN "Shorty" Structure — Bradley Pounds / HomeBuyerSchool.com

**Deck:** 60+ Programs for High-Earning First-Time Buyers (evergreen / on-demand replay cut)
**Source:** live webinar, 78 slides / 107 embedded images (`brad_slides.pdf` + `brad_slides.txt`)
**Funnel:** cold FB (Housing Special Ad Category, broad-only) → registration → **on-demand webinar** → BOOK A CALL → high-ticket homebuyer coaching
**Offer:** FREE STRATEGY CALL (soft, book-a-call). No price / guarantee / hard-scarcity stack.
**Target runtime:** ~22 min (band 20-25) vs the 60-90 min live.
**Format:** Fladlien 14-section spine + cinema pacing, self-running / narrated (auto-advance = evergreen mode).
**Skill:** slidev_presentation V-07 v3.1. Rules in force: 1 idea/slide · ≤7 visible words on hooks (notes carry script) · v-clicks on every 2+ list · NO em-dashes · NO emoji in body (Rule 13 → Iconify/text) · NO mid-slide fake buttons (Rule 11; page-level CTA button below the video is fine) · content-fits-frame (Rule 9) · `colorSchema` pinned (Rule 12).

## 0. KEEP / COMPRESS / CUT — VERDICT HEADLINE

> **Keep the belief spine (6 Ahas) and the 4 sticky hooks — Mack-truck loophole, the Slime-Green / Apartment-Beige 100%-financing map, the Secret-Ninja 401k, and "you qualify even as a high earner." Compress the entire 60+ program catalog to ONE block: "here are the 4 categories + our favorites — we cover all 60+ on the call." Cut every live-chat prompt, the "quick check-in," the deep per-program spec sheets (USDA maps, VA funding fees, TSAHC lien mechanics), and all "tonight / one hour" language. Result: 78 slides → ~50 slides, ~22 minutes.**

The catalog is where a FTHB webinar bloats. In the live version the program tour earns its length because Brad is performing live and the room wants the tour. On an evergreen shorty the tour is where viewers drop. The move: **name the categories, flash the two unforgettable visuals (the shaded map + the 401k ninja), promise the full 60+ on the call.** The catalog stops being the teaching and becomes the reason to book.

## 4. CTA DESIGN — on-demand replay (book-a-call)

**Rule 11 compliance:** no fake clickable button painted mid-slide. But this is a replay **page**, not a Zoom share — so the real affordances live on the page around the video, and the in-deck CTA slide uses crystal-clear, honest patterns.

**Page layout (replay page, outside the deck canvas):**
- Video player (the self-running deck / narrated MP4).
- **Directly below the video:** a real `BOOK A FREE STRATEGY CALL` button (this is a web page, buttons are legitimately clickable here).
- **QR code** beside/under the button (scannable from a phone watching on desktop).
- **Visible booking URL** in monospace as the type-it-yourself fallback (e.g. `homebuyerschool.com/call`).

**In-deck CTA slide (F7), Rule-11-clean:**
- Headline statement CTA: "Book your free strategy call." (a statement, not a styled button).
- **QR code in a white card** (~200px, brand-colored dark modules, verified to decode, Cloudinary-hosted) pointing to the booking link.
- Visible URL in monospace below the QR.
- Speaker note instructs: the booking link/button lives below the video on the replay page; narration points the viewer there.

**Value-stack reframe (no price — book-a-call economics):**
The "stack" is not a priced bundle; it's the set of things booking/watching-through unlocks. Reframe the O6 "stay till end" stack as the **booking incentive**:

| The free call gets you | Source |
|---|---|
| A **personalized program match** — which of the 60+ actually fit your income/credit/goal (the thing the shorty deliberately did NOT cover) | L420-432 payoff |
| The free book: **The Texas First-Time Homebuyer Blueprint** | L42-43 |
| The **full 65-program list** in your inbox | L44 |
| A **$50 Amazon gift card** for booking + attending | L47 |
| A **free soft credit pull** (normally $30+) | L172-173 |

Framing line: "The call is free. You walk away with the book, the full list, and a plan built for YOUR numbers — whether or not we ever work together."
