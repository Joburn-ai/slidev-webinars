# REFERENCE NOTES — the craft to steal, and the gap it exposes in ours

**Source:** John's reference video (`youtube.com/watch?v=uNojYrhYoDc`), analysed frame by
frame with Gemini 2.5 Pro. Plus the NHB+ doc (`12wr2KOtqH...`).

**John's own read, and it is the reason these notes exist:**

> *"That's probably one area we have been a little bit weak with our own... it's almost a
> little bit too traditional VSL, which is going to fall a little bit flat on some people."*

**He is right, and the analysis says exactly why.** Everything below is the specific
mechanism that makes the reference read as a training rather than a pitch.

---

# 1. 🔴 THE SINGLE BIGGEST LESSON: THE TOOL IS THE HERO, NOT THE GURU

**He spends most of the runtime inside the board and the spreadsheets. The SYSTEM is the
star.** That one choice moves the frame from *"buy from me"* to *"copy my system."*

**A traditional VSL tells you what to believe. This tells you WHY things work** -- at 7:24
he explains that people ignore outreach because of *pattern recognition*, not because they
hate offers. **That is a pedagogical frame, and it is what makes him a teacher instead of
a seller.**

**Test to apply to every one of our slides:** is the hero of this slide the mechanism, or
is it us? **If it is us, we have drifted into VSL register.**

---

# 2. THE REHOOK CADENCE IS FRONT-LOADED. OURS IS NOT.

**Measured from the reference:**

| Window | Rehook every |
|---|---|
| 0-60 sec | **10-20 seconds** |
| 1-2 min | **30-40 seconds** |
| after 2 min | slows, because **the value itself holds attention** |

🔴 **Our spec says "every 60-90 seconds" uniformly. That is wrong at the top of the
asset**, which is exactly where attention is cheapest to lose. **Fix: three to four
rehooks inside the first minute.**

**And note the second half of that finding: after two minutes he stops needing rehooks,
because he is handing over real things.** A rehook is what you use when you have nothing
concrete to give. **Density of value is the superior attention device.**

## Rehook devices he actually uses, verbatim

- **Curiosity gap:** *"Most strategies failed. But one worked insanely well."* (0:11)
- **Cost of inaction:** *"Skip this, and you'll spend the next few months testing things that we already proved won't work."* (0:41)
- **Contrast promise:** *"But here is what one week of outreach looks like when done right."* (1:25)
- **Proof pledge:** *"By step 4 you'll literally be booking one call every single day. I'll prove it to you."* (1:36)
- **New gap mid-teach:** *"Now here's the interesting part. Most replies don't come from the first Loom. They come from the follow-ups."* (5:01)

## Micro-hook shape, consistent throughout

> **Conclude the previous point → state the next logical step.**

*"So let's get into it."* · *"Well, I can tell you it's not."* · *"So let me show you exactly how we do this."* · *"Now, everything you've seen so far only works if you track it properly."*

# 3. PATTERN INTERRUPTS — and ours are missing the important kind

**He interrupts with FORMAT CHANGES, not just animation:**

| Time | Interrupt | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| 1:26 | 86 seconds of static board, then a **hard cut to a packed colour-coded calendar** | visual shock **that is simultaneously the proof** of the claim he just made |
| 1:34 | **zoom out to reveal the ENTIRE board** | "this is comprehensive" + frames him as the guide who will simplify it |
| 1:50, 2:14 | **draws a line** under a key phrase | tiny action, pulls the eye, marks the load-bearing point |
| 2:51 | **board → live browser**, a real Instagram page | grounds theory in reality. Stops being a lecture |
| 8:00 | **board → talking head close-up** to explain the psychology | register change from tactical to strategic. Signals meta-commentary, builds trust |

🔴 **We have zero format changes planned.** A 24-32 minute deck that never leaves the
slide surface is the definition of "too traditional." **Minimum to add: one live-screen
segment and one talking-head segment**, both at register changes rather than at random.

---

# 4. THE BOARD IS A MAP, NOT A SLIDE DECK

- 🔴 **Reveal, do not build.** The board is **pre-built** and revealed whole at 1:34 as a visual table of contents. **The viewer always knows where they are and where they are going.**
- **Zoom is the transition.** Zoom out for relationships between steps, zoom in for one detail. **That is his primary navigation, and it is more dynamic than flipping slides.**
- **Spatial linearity:** four clear columns, Step 1 → 4. Complexity made manageable by layout alone.

**What to copy:** the map-reveal, then zoom-guided focus.

**What NOT to copy:** *"the sheer density of his board is on the edge of being
overwhelming"* and *"his freehand drawing is a bit sloppy."* **Ours should be sparser and
the annotation cleaner** -- which lines up with John's "doesn't need to be retro
futuristic."

---

# 5. 🔴 THE SPECIFIC VALUE THROWS — THE ENGINE, AND OUR REAL GAP

**John:** *"What he does well is actually give some clear tangible and specific value
throw... wherever we can, weave in additional examples, social proof, specific numbers,
results, behind-the-scenes stuff. That's going to be super super super important."*

**What the reference actually hands over:**

| Type | Example from the video |
|---|---|
| **Exact figures, not rounded** | *"sent **162,513** messages"* · *"book **5,940** calls this year from outbound alone"* · *"scale to **$80k/mo**"* |
| **Named tools** | IG: Growman · YT: Similartube · LinkedIn: PhantomBuster. **Usable in the next five minutes** |
| **A real named example** | pulls up an actual profile as the model lead source |
| **A live demo** | screencast of the scrape, start to finish |
| **A real file** | the downloaded CSV on screen |
| **Real test data** | five openers with their actual reply rates: **4%, 16%, 7%, 21%, 3%** |
| **Exact parameters** | the real keyword and follower-count filters in the sheet |
| **A portable framework** | the 4-part Loom structure: Hook, Challenge, Relevant Value, Response |
| **Word-for-word copy** | a complete hook script, and the actual text of a winning email opener |

**Note `162,513` and `5,940`.** Not "160k", not "about 6,000". **The unrounded digit IS the
proof** -- a round number reads as an estimate, an exact one reads as a record. This
independently confirms John's ruling: *specific to the penny, including in the titles.*

## 🔴 The balance that makes it work

> **Hyper-specific about the HOW-TO. Vague about the scaled implementation.**

**Specific:** exact tools, exact structures, exact copy, exact KPIs, exact numbers.
**Deliberately vague:** how to become a top closer · deliverability internals · the full
library of winning openers · the contents of the care package.

**That is the whole trick.** Give away the method completely; keep the scaled library and
the execution. **Nobody feels sold to, and the demand is created by the gap.**

# 6. THE CTA IS LOW-FRICTION AND NOT A PURCHASE

**The ask is a DM with a specific phrase.** It reads as *gaining access to a private
resource library*, not a transaction, and it moves the prospect into a one-to-one
conversation where the real sale happens later.

**Ours is already close** -- *"get your Roadmap"* is a low-friction ask. **Keep it that
way. Do not let it drift into a booking demand.**

---

# 7. WHAT NOT TO COPY

- **The arm cast.** A genuine humanising accident. **Manufacturing a flaw reads as fake.**
- **"I'll send you $100" style stunts.** The principle (bold risk reversal) is sound; **the execution has to match a restrained operator brand** or it reads gimmicky.
- **The board's extreme density.** Show a complete system, not every possible node.
- **Casual-to-the-point-of-sloppy production.** His unpolished look buys authenticity; ours buys doubt if the argument is about precision.

---

# 8. FROM THE NHB+ DOC — two lines sharper than what we had

Mostly reinforces `DOCTRINE_absolution_and_timeline_language`. **Two formulations are
sharper than our current versions:**

1. **"Your beliefs are justifications, and justifications are explanations of limitations."** A cleaner statement of why arguing with a belief fails: you are arguing with an explanation, not a cause.
2. **"Every single goal that you set, that you did not get, changes your core identity."** This is the mechanism behind the Stage Trap. Every good product bought for the wrong stage is a goal set and missed, and it **cost identity, not just money.** That is why the self-blame line in B9 lands.

**Plus the frame we already hold, restated:** the product *solves* a limitation; **the
marketing *absolves* them of it.** And *away language is away-from-LOSS language*, because
the subconscious is protecting against loss -- so **all behavioural friction exists to
stop further loss.**
