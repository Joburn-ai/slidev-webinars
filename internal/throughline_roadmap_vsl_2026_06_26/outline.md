## Pacing zones (400 Slide Hack, mini scale)
For a ~4–6 min mini, target **~50–90 slides** (vs 250–400 for a full webinar): one idea/one image per slide, fast cuts. Density by zone (per `ref_fladlien_framework.md`):
- **Hook/Pain (1–2):** fastest cuts, heaviest emotional imagery.
- **Mechanisms (7):** slowest, most build-animations, the two whiteboard holds.
- **Offer→Scarcity (10–14):** medium, proof + clarity, no clutter.
