# CONTENT MAP -- which section produces which content unit

**One roadmap build produces eleven content units.** That is the point of building it in Slidev
rather than as a PDF: the deck is the source, and the cuts are already marked.

| # | From | Unit | Length | Format | Needs a real prospect? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title | "A map needs two points" | 30 sec | Reel, talking head | No |
| 2 | §0 Constraints | "The question nobody asks before giving you a plan" | 45 sec | Reel | No |
| 3 | §1 Snapshot | "Reading someone their own words back" | 60 sec | Screen-record, anonymised | Yes, blurred |
| 4 | §2 Common belief | "Most people think X. It's actually Y" | 30 sec | Reel, highest reposts | No |
| 5 | §2 Damaging admission | The admission, said straight to camera | 45 sec | Reel | No |
| 6 | §3 Four options | "Four ways to fix this and the fatal flaw in each" | 60-90 sec | Carousel or reel | No |
| 7 | §4 Mechanism (drauu) | "The whole mechanism in 90 seconds" | 90 sec | Screen-record of the live draw | No |
| 8 | §4 Timeline | "Why your timeline should not look like mine" | 45 sec | Reel | No |
| 9 | §6 Investment | "Cost of inaction first, price last" | 60 sec | Reel, teaches the frame | No |
| 10 | §6 Crossroads | "Two ways this goes" | 30 sec | Reel, most re-postable | Yes, numbers only |
| 11 | Whole deck | The training-first long-form | 15-20 min | YouTube | No |

## The rules

**Nine of eleven need no prospect at all.** Build those from the generic template and they are
publishable the day the template is filled once.

**The two that do need a prospect need permission, and the permission ask is already a gate.**
Checkpoint 3 asks "would you recommend this to a peer" -- add one line: *"is it okay if we use
your results in our content?"* If yes, unit 3 and unit 10 become publishable, and the same
recording serves as the DM back to them.

**The long-form is not a cut, it is the whole thing presented.** Same deck, generic example
instead of a real prospect, gates stripped, no pitch at the end. Speaker notes are the script.

## Order to produce them

1. **Unit 7 first** (the mechanism draw). It is the hardest to fake, the most useful, and it
   proves the whole thing is real.
2. **Then 4, 5, 9** -- the three that need nothing but a camera and teach the frames the offer
   rests on.
3. **Then 11**, the long-form, once the individual beats have been said out loud a few times and
   are tight.
4. **Then 1, 2, 6, 8** as fill.
5. **3 and 10 last**, when a real prospect has said yes.

**Do not produce all eleven before publishing any.** One rip a day, six days a week, is the
locked cadence -- Metricool's 2026 study across a million accounts found reach fell 35% while
volume doubled industry-wide, so eleven at once buys nothing and costs the week.
